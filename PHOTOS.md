Refresh the site and it shows up. A missing photo just keeps the icon, so you can add them one at a time.

Keep each file **under about 300 KB** (compress at squoosh.app or tinyjpg.com) so pages stay fast.

## Where to get good photos (free and safe to use)
- **Unsplash** (unsplash.com), **Pexels** (pexels.com), **Pixabay** (pixabay.com): free for commercial use, no credit required. Read each photo's licence note.
- Your own phone photos of real products, markets and people in Ghana will always look the most authentic.
- **Do not** use photos copied from other websites (Jiji, Google Images, etc.). They belong to someone else.
- If a photo shows a recognisable person, only use it if it's from a free stock site, or you have their permission.

## Tips
- Landscape photos for hero and promos, with the subject on the right side.
- Bright, sharp, uncluttered. Avoid photos with text or logos in them.
- After adding a photo, hold Ctrl and press F5 if the old version still shows.
